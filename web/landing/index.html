Features

Everything you need to find a moment.

Plain-English search

Ask the way you would ask a colleague.

Proof with every clip

A red box, timestamps and measures show why it matched.

Daily footage library per camera

Each camera keeps its own day-by-day recordings.

Zones you draw on a still frame

Mark an area once and ask about it.

Saved clips

Keep the moments you need to come back to.

Any object you can name

Boxes, pallets, bikes: if you can name it, you can ask.

Works with what you already have

  • IP cameras (RTSP)
  • MP4 and MOV uploads
  • Daily recording folders
  • Your own database

Example scenarios

Warehouse loading dock

You could ask

"Show me when a pallet was loaded onto the truck."

What you get

The clip of the loading, the time range, and the pallet outlined in red.

Retail shop floor

You could ask

"Show me when someone stood at the shelf."

What you get

The matching moments, each with the area and the time it happened.

Office entrance

You could ask

"Show me when a person walked through the door."

What you get

A short clip for each time it happened, with the evidence beside it.

Illustrative examples, not customer testimonials.

Privacy and control

No face recognition. No identity features.

  • Footage stays in your library.
  • The camera password is checked and never stored.
  • You decide what is indexed.

Cost

Pay once to index. Search is free.

About $0.20 per 1,000 frames per object type.

Indexing uses the 5 fps copy of your footage. After that, every search costs nothing.
